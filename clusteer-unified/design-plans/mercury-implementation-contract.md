# Clusteer website implementation contract

This local pass implements the accepted comparative audit on codex/mercury-inspired-design-revamp. It extends the approved Mercury-inspired identity and does not deploy or change the pricing formula.

## Motion

One bounded sticky scene, landscape → shared customer conversion interface → transfer → receipt. The backdrop is the original static generated estuary, transformed and faded with scroll; this is not a scrubbed video or Mercury asset. Financial text is HTML. Native scroll events schedule one animation frame; media-query and ResizeObserver updates fit the frame. Small/short viewports and reduced motion get normal flow, identical content, and manual controls. No transaction calls exist in the public demo.

## Public information

Products, Company and Resources navigation; direct How it works; buy/sell routes prominent; planned features explicitly labelled. Shared Help/FAQ content reconciles custody, price and availability claims. Fees and Safety pages explain the quote and fund flow without inventing a tariff or legal classification.

## Open operational decisions

- Confirm the production rate source and approved tariff; rate endpoint applies premiums while old copy claimed no markup.
- Confirm the exact asset/network/bank availability matrix, limits and settlement expectations.
- Confirm publication-ready partner details and any regulatory statements with the product owner.
- Verify payment requests, app distribution, market rates and alerts before moving them from in-development status.

No unknown above blocks an honest local website preview. They remain release-content decisions, not facts invented by this pass.

## User preference

Fully rounded buttons apply to the website only. Dashboard buttons retain their current shape.
